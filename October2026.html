More Engagement Information

  • Women Pharmacist Day Lecture Monday 10/12
  • Togetherall – Free, Anonymous 24/7 Peer Support for Students

    Togetherall is a free and anonymous peer support program that is free for all UIC students to use. It is all online for easy access and no wait lists. Togetherall is monitored by mental health professionals 24/7 to help support whatever you may need.
  • A Myaamia Artist’s Map of Chicago
    By Megan Sekulich (Miami Tribe of Oklahoma)

    Chicago is, and has always been, an Indigenous place. Using the waterways of šikaakonki “Chicago” and northern Illinois as an outline, the space between each river is filled with native flora and fauna, many of which are culturally significant to myaamiaki “Myaamia people.”

    Megan also created a linework-only version of the graphic as a coloring activity, which you can download here .
