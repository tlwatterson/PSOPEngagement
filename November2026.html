Resource Spotlight: A Connected Student-Support Network

  • Mental health and consultation: UIC Counseling Center provides clinical and non-clinical services, including crisis intervention, brief services for eligible students, workshops, drop-in spaces, and faculty and staff consultation.
  • Access and accommodations: The Disability Resource Center supports full access and engagement, develops accommodation plans, and provides guidance for instructors receiving Letters of Accommodation.
  • Complex circumstances and navigation: The Office of the Dean of Students supports students experiencing crises, life trauma, academic barriers, basic-needs concerns, and other challenges.
  • Wellness and basic needs: The UIC Wellness Center offers wellness education, consultations, workshops, the Flames Food Pantry, and other basic-needs resources.
